A-Tags: 151 tags, 151 follies

Lay printed tags on a table and tap "Use camera". Every tag the machine can read grows its own folly. On a phone the view opens full screen with the rear camera. Until then, a simulated camera runs the same reader on six tags.

Starting…
    1. Turn the frame into black and white, comparing each pixel with its neighbourhood.
    2. Find dark squares and their four corners. Thin grey outlines show every candidate.
    3. Straighten each square and read its 10 × 10 grid of cells.
    4. Compare the grid with all 151 tags in four rotations. When two tags are almost equally close, the label says so with a question mark, and the folly appears as a ghost.
    5. From the four corners, estimate the camera pose and grow the folly out of its card.
